Ashwin joined the club under mysterious circumstances and has remained its most talked-about, least reliable asset ever since. Known league-wide for his post-match interviews that are somehow longer and more dramatic than the match itself.

Scouts describe his game as "unpredictable" — mainly because nobody can predict whether he'll actually show up on time. Off the pitch, he is considered a world-class talent in the art of the confident explanation for why the ball definitely should have gone in.

"I've never seen someone celebrate a corner kick like it was a World Cup final." — Anonymous teammate
